Request School Access

Every school must formally request access from Generali before it can be activated on DSAMS.

Approval required
Mandatory onboarding rule: Submitting a request does not automatically create an active school account. Generali, as the central platform administrator, must review the school's details, verify its identity and approve access. Only then can the school tenant and authorised administrator accounts be provisioned.

School application for DSAMS access

1. Request

School submits official details and authorised contact information.

2. Verification

Generali verifies the school, its authority and the requester's role.

3. Approval

Central administrator approves or declines with a recorded reason.

4. Secure activation

Approved school receives a tenant workspace and setup instructions. School admin activates MFA and invites staff.

Accounts & Payments

Two distinct school finance areas plus the proposed DSAMS platform charge.

No live payments

Tuition / approved school fee invoices, balances, receipts, payment references, arrears and reconciliation.

Ledger A

🧾 Other school charges

Separately itemised approved items such as meals, transport, activities, boarding, uniforms or learning materials, according to school policy.

Ledger B

Proposed DSAMS platform service fee

The approved product requirement for this edition is KSh 20 per registered parent / guardian account, per school term. Each parent account renews its access payment every term while registered and using DSAMS. The charge is per parent account, not per learner, and is separate from school fees and other school charges.

The charging interval is termly: KSh 20 per parent account for each term. Before live collection, publish the term dates, payment due date, grace period, access status after non-payment, exemptions, refunds and dispute process. Obtain appropriate legal, tax, child-data and payment-provider review. This demo does not collect money.

Payment controls

  • Use a vetted payment provider; never store card PINs, mobile-money PINs or raw credentials.
  • Unique transaction references and idempotency prevent duplicate posting.
  • Webhook signature verification and reconciliation before marking invoices paid.
  • Separate school funds and platform revenue in accounting and reporting.
  • Receipts, refunds, reversals, approvals and immutable audit records.
